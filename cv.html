2.Research

Quantum information: networks, error correction, and fault diagnosis. Research in heterogeneous quantum repeater architectures, heralded erasures, asynchronous network QEC, graph-state fault diagnosis, geometric structure of exact erasure correction, and statistical memory diagnostics for QEC syndrome records at UMass Amherst. 2026–present.

Trapped-ion electric-field noise and geometry. Honors thesis and preprint work using Green-function kernels, enclosure geometry, spatial correlations, and billiard return spectra to study anomalous electric-field noise in ion traps, advised by Chris Cox and Robert Niffenegger. 2025–2026.

Randomized numerical linear algebra. Randomized acceleration of contour-integral eigensolvers, culminating in the RA-FEAST preprint. 2025.
