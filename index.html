Ayush Nadiger

I study fault-tolerant quantum computing in distributed systems, focusing on quantum error correction and entanglement generation over noisy links. I am applying to PhD programs for Fall 2027.

I am an ECE master's student at UMass Amherst and a graduate research assistant under Filip Rozpędek in the Quantum Information Lab (QuaIL). My thesis is currently chaired by Don Towsley in the ACQuIRE lab. I work on hybrid repeaters with Stav Haldar at UT Arlington.

Projects

Scalable Entanglement Generation with Encoded Hybrid Repeaters in 2D Networks

2026 – Present

Encoded hybrid repeaters, placement, and routing for scalable entanglement generation in two-dimensional quantum networks. This is graduate research with Filip Rozpędek and Stav Haldar, presented as a poster at the CQN site visit (October 2026).

Asynchronous Quantum Error Correction

2026 – Present

Error correction and syndrome information when distributed operations depend on entanglement arriving at different times.

Differential Geometry of Quantum Erasure Correction

2025

The geometry of exact erasure-correcting code subspaces, their perturbations, and information leakage. A Differential Geometry course project.

Statistical Memory Tests for Quantum Error-Correction Records

2025 – 2026

Predictive-rank tests for temporal structure in syndrome records, with an application to public Google data. An Applied Multivariate Statistics course project.

Geometry-Controlled Noise in Trapped-Ion Systems

2025 – 2026

How electrode geometry shapes electric-field noise and spatial correlations in trapped-ion systems. This was my Undergraduate Honors Thesis. Submitted to Physical Review A. arXiv preprint.

The Bond Repair Problem in Graph States

2025 – 2026

Distinguishing a missing entangling gate from endpoint-memory dephasing using product measurements. Manuscript. Code.
