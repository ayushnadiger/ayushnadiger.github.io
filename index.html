Department of Electrical & Computer Engineering

Abstract. I work on quantum information, especially quantum networks and quantum error correction. I am interested in architectures where hardware is heterogeneous, resources arrive asynchronously, and the useful question is not merely whether an operation is possible, but what physical structure makes it worthwhile. Earlier work of mine includes trapped-ion electric-field noise and randomized algorithms for large eigenvalue problems. I begin an M.S. in ECE at UMass Amherst in Fall 2026.
