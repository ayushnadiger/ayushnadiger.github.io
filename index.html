Electrical & Computer Engineering · University of Massachusetts Amherst

Research. I study quantum error correction and fault-tolerant architectures under physical hardware constraints. How should a quantum network be designed when memories decay, gates are imperfect, and entanglement arrives at random times?

I am an M.S. student and graduate research assistant with Filip Rozpędek at UMass Amherst. My work combines mathematical analysis and simulation, building on earlier research on electric-field noise in trapped-ion devices.

Email· Scholar· GitHub· CV

1.Research directions

Sparse quantum repeater architectures

When can a small number of better memories and error-correcting nodes deliver the performance of a much more expensive network? I study hardware requirements, placement, routing, and synchronization.

Asynchronous quantum error correction

QEC and decoding when qubits have different ages and entanglement links succeed at different times. Current work examines heralding information, syndrome timing, and the cost of waiting.

Device geometry and correlated noise

How electrode geometry shapes electric-field noise and spatial correlations in ion traps, using Green-function models and numerical geometry diagnostics.
