Research Papers Notes CV

Department of Electrical & Computer Engineering
University of Massachusetts Amherst

Abstract. I work on quantum information, especially quantum error correction and quantum networks. Current projects include heterogeneous repeater architectures, repair diagnosis in graph states, differential geometry of exact erasure correction, and statistical memory diagnostics for repeated syndrome records. Earlier work includes trapped-ion electric-field noise and randomized algorithms for large eigenvalue problems. I begin an M.S. in ECE at UMass Amherst in Fall 2026.

1.Current work

QEC / networks

Hybrid quantum repeaters

Entanglement distribution when only a sparse subset of repeater nodes has high-quality memory and local error correction.

QEC / graph states

Repair identifiability and product-measurement complexity

Which graph-state fault mechanisms can be distinguished from product-Pauli data, and how measurement context, retained statistics, Pauli weight, and noise constrain repair diagnosis.

QEC / geometry

Covariance geometry of linearized quantum erasure correction

Grassmannian geometry of the exact Knill–Laflamme locus, covariance-controlled normal structure, leakage Hessians, and code rigidity.

QEC / statistics

Predictive rank as a memory witness for QEC syndrome records

Circuit separator bounds, finite-sample rank tests, and conditional model checking on public Google surface-code records.

trapped ions

Geometry-controlled correlated electric-field noise in enclosed ion traps

Geometry-dependent correlated electric-field noise, enclosure effects, Green-function kernels, and billiard return spectra in trapped-ion devices.
