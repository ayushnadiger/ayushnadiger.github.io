Quantum scarring and topological diagnostics

exploratory note · spectral geometry

Abstract. Exploratory work on whether topology extracted from billiard eigenfunctions can distinguish ordinary chaotic structure from concentration associated with unstable periodic orbits. This was a side project, not my honors thesis.

1.Question

A scar is visually compelling but "looks concentrated along an orbit" is not itself a statistic. One possible approach is to threshold an eigenfunction, track connected components or persistent features across levels, and compare those summaries against nearby eigenstates or randomized controls.

2.Status

This page records an exploratory direction and associated code. I keep it because the geometric questions are interesting, but it should not be read as a completed result or as the trapped-ion thesis.
