CP³, the Segre embedding, and concurrence

expository note · geometry / quantum information

Abstract. A pure two-qubit state is a point of complex projective three-space. Product states form the Segre quadric, and the familiar determinant condition for separability becomes a geometric equation. These notes collect that picture and relate it to concurrence.

1.Setup

Write a pure state as coefficients of a 2×2 matrix. Projectivizing removes the physically irrelevant overall complex scale. The separable locus is then exactly the rank-one locus, which is the image of CP¹ × CP¹ under the Segre embedding into CP³.

2.Concurrence

For a normalized pure state, concurrence is twice the absolute determinant of the coefficient matrix. Algebraically it vanishes on the Segre quadric; geometrically it measures departure from the product-state locus. The phrase “distance” requires care because the numerical value depends on the chosen metric and normalization.
