Randomized-Accelerated FEAST

A. Nadiger · numerical linear algebra · 2025

[arXiv:2512.01257]

Abstract. RA-FEAST combines contour-integral eigensolvers with randomized numerical linear algebra for partial eigendecomposition of large sparse matrices. Randomized subspace initialization permits more aggressive quadrature and truncated refinement while retaining high accuracy on the tested problems.

1.Idea

FEAST obtains an eigenspace by applying a rational approximation to a spectral projector. The expensive part is repeatedly solving shifted linear systems along the contour. RA-FEAST asks whether a good randomized starting subspace can reduce the amount of contour work required before refinement.

2.Results

The preprint gives a probabilistic warm-start error bound, a perturbation result for inexact FEAST iterations, and sparse graph-Laplacian experiments. In the reported benchmark regime, the randomized variant reached speedups of up to 38× while preserving accurate approximations to the target eigenspace.
