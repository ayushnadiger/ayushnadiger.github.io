Asynchronous quantum error correction

Question. What syndrome information and error-correcting guarantees survive while a distributed transversal CNOT is assembled from entanglement arriving at different times?

Results. I derived an exact formula for the local-syndrome deficit of a partial CSS transversal CNOT and a rate–distance bound that holds for every coordinate order. I also established an arbitrary-order deferred-recovery guarantee for coordinate-confined Pauli faults and built exact stabilizer and Pauli-frame tests. Recent tests expose schedules in which individually recoverable pauses accumulate an unresolved logical ambiguity.

Status. Manuscript in preparation. The algebraic results and idealized tests are distinct from the ongoing work on noisy extraction circuits and performance at matched physical resources.

Hybrid quantum repeater architectures

Question. Can a network mix many simple repeater nodes with sparse, higher-quality nodes to outperform a uniform architecture?

Built. With Filip Rozpędek and Stav Haldar, I reproduced the one-dimensional chain benchmark from the supplied simulation code and developed hardware-dependent spacing estimates. The model combines highly multiplexed, shorter-lived memories (e.g., diamond color centers or atomic-frequency-comb memories) with longer-lived, lower-error memories (e.g., trapped ions or neutral atoms) at hybrid nodes. The analysis connects memory coherence and gate errors to correction spacing, fidelity, and secret-key rate.

Status. Ongoing collaborative research. The chain baseline supports extensions to two-dimensional placement, routing, and multiple paths; those extensions are under validation.

Trapped-ion noise and geometry

Question. How does an electrode enclosure change electric-field noise and spatial correlations when the surface-noise spectrum is fixed?

Results. I derived Green-function geometry factors, exact enclosure laws for controlled geometries, and spatial noise correlations. Numerical geometry diagnostics connect the electrostatic response to billiard return spectra. The work grew out of my honors thesis with Christopher Cox and Robert Niffenegger.

Status. Public preprint, with code and numerical data.

Repair identifiability in graph states

Question. Can a measurement record identify a fault while hiding the information needed to repair it?

Results. I characterized the Pauli witnesses that distinguish a severed bond from endpoint-memory dephasing and showed why target-stabilizer statistics can discard the repair label. A verification suite checks the witness and measurement-resource claims on finite graphs.

Status. Manuscript in preparation. Theorems and examples · Verification code

Predictive rank of QEC syndrome records

Question. What can finite syndrome records reveal about noise memory once the circuit's own contribution is accounted for?

Results. I derived a circuit/noise separator bound on predictive rank and finite-sample tests. Applying the diagnostics to public distance-5 surface-code records identifies a finite-horizon discrepancy from the supplied circuit model.

Status. Manuscript in preparation; the empirical result is a conditional model check, without identifying a microscopic noise mechanism. Theory and data analysis

Covariance geometry of erasure correction

Question. How does the set of exactly correctable code subspaces sit inside the Grassmannian?

Results. I derived a covariance-controlled normal form for the linearized Knill–Laflamme constraints. Exact symbolic calculations establish infinitesimal rigidity modulo local unitaries for the stated erasure constraints on the [[4,2,2]] and [[5,1,3]] codes.

Status. Manuscript in preparation, with a public verification suite. Derivations and verification
