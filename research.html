Research

Current questions first; older and exploratory work is kept in the archive rather than made to look more finished than it is.

1.Quantum networks

I study entanglement distribution in heterogeneous repeater networks: some nodes are cheap and noisy, while a sparse subset can store qubits longer or perform local error correction. The interesting regime is therefore neither an ideal fully error-corrected network nor a uniform chain. It is the intermediate architecture, where routing, waiting, local processing, and classical metadata all interact.

2.Quantum error correction for networks

A second line of work concerns heralded erasures and asynchronous information in network QEC. The physical setting matters: losses can be known, entanglement does not arrive on a common clock, and high-quality memory may be scarce. I am interested in what code structure and decoding information remain useful once those constraints are treated as part of the model rather than averaged away.

current · work in progress

3.Trapped-ion noise and geometry

My honors thesis studied anomalous electric-field noise in ion traps through geometry. Instead of treating electrode distance as the only scale, I used finite-element Green-function kernels and billiard-style geometric diagnostics to ask how boundaries recycle or redirect field-noise contributions. The resulting picture separates nearly surface-like geometries from channel-like geometries whose multi-boundary structure changes both noise strength and anisotropy.

project page · MassURC abstract

4.Randomized numerical linear algebra

RA-FEAST explores whether randomized subspace information can reduce the cost of contour-integral eigensolvers without giving up the accuracy that makes FEAST attractive. The project combines randomized initialization, inexact refinement, error analysis, and sparse numerical experiments.

project page · arXiv:2512.01257
